Writing help

Accept the next suggested word.

Request another suggestion.

Accept the number of words chosen in Settings.
Alt + Enter
Accept the whole displayed suggestion.
Esc
Dismiss a suggestion or close a panel.
Ctrl/Cmd + S
Save your document on this device.

Suggestion shortcuts apply only in the focused editor while a suggestion is available. Every acceptance is one undo step. Unaccepted words are not saved or learned.

In Settings choose Imported Texts, My Writing or Combined. Sources → Use selects the files to combine. My Writing contributes only with learning consent and an explicit Save.

Maximum N includes every smaller order. Each usable N nominates its top X words after source counts are added; each admitted count is multiplied by its N-gram setting (0–250×), equal words have these virtual scores summed, and the final top X is sampled by score. Zero-weight orders do not contribute; if all usable orders have zero weight, no suggestion is shown. Stored counts never change when adjusting a multiplier. Later preview words use earlier suggested words as virtual context.
